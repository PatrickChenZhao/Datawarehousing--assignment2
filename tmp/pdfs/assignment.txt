       Project  Overview                                                          
        This assessment is designed to assess the following learning outcomes:    
                                                                                  
        1. Critically reflect on in-demand data warehouse designs, their role in a decision-making
                                                                                  
          process, advantages and potential problem areas.                        
        2. Critically appraise data modelling options to design data warehouse schemas using
          contemporary patterns, including but not limited to Star and Snowflake. 
        3. Understand and demonstrate knowledge of advanced SQL techniques to access,
          aggregate and analyse data from big relational databases.               
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
        In a nutshell, it consists of two sections. In the first section, you need to design and implement
        a data warehouse. In the second section, you will write some queries to answer some specific
        questions based on a given schema.                                        
                                                                                  
       1.1 Group Work                                                             
                                                                                  
       This is a Group Assignment (two members max). Liaise with your classmates and create your
       team of two people. Notes:                                                 
                                                                                  
          (a) Students can choose to work individually; there will be NO penalty. In other words, this
            Project will be marked up to the same standards and marking criteria, regardless of
            whether you work alone or in a group of two students.                 
                                                                                  
          (b) Please form a group on Canvas by yourself by 2 Oct. Alternatively, email name/s
            and student/s ID of your team (one or two students) to Ryan (ryan.ip@aut.ac.nz). You
            will be assumed to be working individually if you did not sign up or indicate your group.

       that can store and analyse data for decision-making. Specifically, the data manager at the
       headquarter would like to oversee the performance of different stores, and be able to analyse
       customers’ behaviour.

       A.3  Schema Design                                                         
                                                                                  
       Because the IT people at PhoneFix have never taken COMP810, they do not know much
       about data warehousing. They have approached you for some suggestions to improve the
       current DW by designing a better schema. By considering their requirements, you are
       required to design a logical schema for the Data Warehouse (DW). You can choose either
       the STAR schema or the SNOWFLAKE schema.                                   
                                                                                  
       Explain the deficiencies of the current DW design used by PhoneFix, justify your choice of
                                                                                  
       schema and the composition of the fact and dimension tables, and explain the comparative
       advantages of your proposed design over the existing one.                  
                                                                                  
       A.3.1 Things to consider                                                   
                                                                                  
       The logical schema for the DW represents aggregated data for specific business activities.
       Using the schema, one can create multiple aggregated data sources that will represent
       different aspects of business operations. For example, the aggregation may involve total
       sales by selected time periods, by product, by client, and so on.          
                                                                                  
       Some aggregated totals to consider are: total products sold, total sales values by product,
       etc. The basic star and snowflake schemas have three main components: facts, dimensions,
       attributes. Consider what constitutes the fact and dimension tables. To determine the correct
                                                                                  
       dimension tables and attributes, you will need to consider the attributes shown in Figure 2.
       However, you do not need to limit yourself to the attributes shown. Feel free to use your
       imagination and creativity to make your schema more suitable for the client.
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       A.4 Drawing DW Schema                                                      
                                                                                  
       Use a Data Modelling software to draw the DW Schema, such as Microsoft Visio or EDRplus.
       Visio is available in AUT computers. If you use some other Data Modelling software, be aware
       that all required elements of the ERD may not be clearly shown.            
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       A.5 Creating the Tables                                                    
       Hence, create the required tables using Oracle. You do not need to insert any records in the
       tables. Submit the code and the descriptions (using DESC) of all tables created (e.g., by
       screenshots).                                                              
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       A.6 What to submit                                                         
                                                                                  
       To summarise, for this section, you need (1) explanations and justifications of your design

       create the tables, and (4) the descriptions (using DESC) of all tables created. Check the
       rubrics on Canvas for a detailed breakdown of the marks in this section.

       Regulations for GAI tools                                                  
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       What Students Can Do with Generative AI Tools Like ChatGPT:                
                                                                                  
          • Idea Generation: Students can use GenAI to brainstorm ideas or get suggestions to
            choose their report topic. This tool can be beneficial in providing different
            perspectives on a subject. Students are required to search Google Scholar to verify
            any idea provided by the GAI.                                         
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          • Research Assistance: GenAI can provide summaries and explanations of complex
            concepts, which can be beneficial for students in their initial stages of research. It
            can also suggest further reading or resources to deepen their understanding.
            However, all the suggested readings MUST be verified by the student.

          • Technical Support: Students can use GenAI to troubleshoot and seek solutions to
            technical problems they might encounter during their projects. By posing their issues
            as questions, GenAI can offer potential solutions or direct students to relevant
            resources.                                                            
                                                                                  
          • Proofreading & Editing: Students can use GenAI to check their grammar,
            punctuation, and spelling. It can provide suggestions for rephrasing sentences or
            improving the flow of the report.                                     
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
       What Students Should NOT Do with GenAI Tools Like ChatGPT:                 
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          • Plagiarize: Students are not allowed to use the output from GenAI without giving
            proper attribution or without adding their own understanding and analysis. Academic
            integrity is paramount; directly copying from an AI output is considered plagiarism.
            Specifically, students are prohibited from utilising GenAI to produce paragraphs that
            are then directly copied into their reports.                          
                                                                                  
          • Sole Source of Information: GenAI should not be the sole source of information for
            a report. Students should verify any facts or information from GenAI against other
            reliable academic sources, such as Google Scholar.                    
                                                                                  
          • Over-reliance: DO NOT over-rely on GenAI to provide ideas. The tool should be
            used as an assistant, not a substitute, for their own critical thinking and writing skills.
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
          • Unverified Information: Students should not directly take the information provided
            by GenAI, e.g., generate whole paragraphs of related works. It's crucial to verify
            information using reputable, peer-reviewed sources, as GenAI tools can contain
            errors or outdated information.                                       
                                                                                  
       Mandatory Declaration of AI Use (AI Use Log)                               
                                                                                  
       Every submission must include a short AI Use Log in the report appendix (the appendix is
       not counted towards the page limit). The log is required for the submission. For each
       generative AI tool used, state the tool's name, the sections in which it was used, and what it
       was used for (e.g., brainstorming the topic, explaining a concept, or proofreading). If you did
       not use any AI tool, you must still include the log and state that no AI tool was used. The log
       itself is not marked for content, but an honest and specific log is expected. Where the log is
       inconsistent with the submitted report, or where the report shows signs of unverified AI-
                                                                                  
       generated content, the case may be referred under the academic integrity process, and the
       in-person interview described below may be required.                       
                                                                                  
       Suggested format for each entry: Tool (e.g., ChatGPT) | Section(s) used | Purpose | How
       the output was verified or revised.
